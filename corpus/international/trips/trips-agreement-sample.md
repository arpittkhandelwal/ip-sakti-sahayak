## Article 27.1 – Patentable Subject Matter

Patents shall be available for any inventions, whether products or processes, in all fields of technology, provided that they are new, involve an inventive step, and are capable of industrial application.

## Article 27.3(b) – Exclusion of Plants and Animals

Members may exclude from patentability:
- Plants and animals other than micro-organisms
- Essentially biological processes for the production of plants or animals other than non-biological and microbiological processes

**Sui Generis Protection**: Members must provide for the protection of plant varieties either by patents or by an effective sui generis system (India adopted the PPVFRA 2001).

## Doha Declaration on TRIPS and Public Health (2001)

Affirms the right of WTO members to use flexibilities under TRIPS to protect public health and promote access to medicines:
- Freedom to grant compulsory licences (Article 31)
- Right to determine what constitutes a national emergency
- Freedom to establish parallel importation regimes
