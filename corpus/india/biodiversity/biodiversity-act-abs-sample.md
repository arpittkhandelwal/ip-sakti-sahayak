## Section 3 – Prior Approval for Access to Biological Resources

No person shall, without the previous approval of the **National Biodiversity Authority (NBA)**:

1. Obtain any biological resource occurring in India or knowledge associated thereto for research or for commercial utilization or for bio-survey and bio-utilization
2. Transfer the results of any research relating to any biological resources occurring in India

**Exceptions under 2023 Amendment**: Domestic companies (including AYUSH manufacturers) need only give prior intimation to the **State Biodiversity Board (SBB)** rather than obtain NBA approval, provided they:
- Are registered entities under Indian law
- Are not joint ventures with foreign participation exceeding 51%

## Section 7 – Prior Intimation to State Biodiversity Board

Every person who intends to obtain any biological resource for commercial purposes, except those covered under Section 3, shall give prior intimation to the concerned State Biodiversity Board (SBB).

## Section 21 – Benefit Sharing

The NBA shall ensure that the terms and conditions subject to which approval is granted secure equitable sharing of benefits arising out of the use of accessed biological resources, their by-products, innovations and practices associated with their use and applications with the local bodies and in consultation with the local bodies of the area where the biological resources are accessed.

## ABS Compliance Checklist for Ayurveda Companies

1. **Identify biological resources**: List all plant/animal/microbial resources used
2. **Check Section 3 vs Section 7 applicability**: Foreign collaboration → NBA approval; Pure domestic → SBB intimation
3. **Apply to NBA/SBB**: Submit Form I (NBA) or intimation letter (SBB) with product details
4. **Negotiate ABS Agreement**: Agree on monetary and non-monetary benefits
5. **Document benefit sharing**: With specific communities or local bodies (Biodiversity Management Committees)
6. **Register with ABSCH**: For international transactions under Nagoya Protocol

## 2023 Amendment Key Changes

- Domestic AYUSH companies exempted from full NBA approval (Section 3 amended)
- Compounding mechanism introduced for minor violations
- Research and academic access simplified
- Codified regulatory sandbox for biotech startups using Indian biological resources

## Penalties (Section 55)

Violation of Section 3 (unauthorized access): Fine up to **Rs. 1 crore** for first offence; imprisonment up to 3 years or fine up to Rs. 10 lakh for individuals.
